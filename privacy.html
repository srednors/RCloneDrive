Privacy notice

Personal Drive Sync is the owner's personal rclone configuration. This notice describes that configuration and these information pages.

Google Drive access

The configuration uses Google's authorisation process and Google Drive API to read, create, update and delete files as needed to synchronise the owner's chosen folders. Authorisation credentials are stored on the owner's computer, separately from these public pages.

The configuration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to analytics services. Files, local recovery copies and Google Drive copies remain in locations controlled by the owner. Their retention depends on the owner's sync, backup and deletion settings.

The owner can revoke this configuration's access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future access; it does not itself delete existing file copies or backups.

These pages

These pages have no sign-in, forms, analytics, cookies set by the page code, or embedded third-party resources. No Drive files, authorisation credentials or account identifiers are published here.

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security. Its handling of request information is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google's processing of account and Drive information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

For questions about this personal configuration, use the owner support address displayed on its Google authorisation screen.
